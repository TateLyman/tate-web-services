Before scan

  • Primary contact button was below the first mobile screen.
  • Search preview did not mention service area or main service.
  • Three large homepage images slowed the first load.
  • No short FAQ for timing, estimates, or what happens after contact.

Fixed in pass

  • Moved call and quote actions into the first mobile view.
  • Rewrote page title and meta description for clearer search snippets.
  • Compressed obvious oversized images and checked image dimensions.
  • Added starter FAQ copy for owner approval.

Owner decisions

  • Choose which reviews or project photos should be featured.
  • Confirm the exact service area and estimate response time.
  • Decide whether to add pricing ranges or keep pricing quote-only.
