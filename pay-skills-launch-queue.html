Add ExchangeRebel (exchangerebel/toll-apis): 108 x402 data APIs on Solana

Adds ExchangeRebel — a fleet of 108 pay-per-call data and utility APIs for AI agents, live at exchangerebel.com under /toll/*, all gated with x402 on Solana mainnet and settled per call in USDC or SOL. Coverage, as enumerated by the service tags in the live discovery document (https://exchangerebel.com/openapi.json), includes: SEC fundamentals, SEC filing feed, SEC Form 4 insider transactions, SEC 13F institutional holdings, and SEC alerts; FINRA short interest; ETF distributions; stock screening; perpetual funding rates; token safety; macro & civic series and US Treasury rates; election finance (FEC, including FEC committees); GDELT news; weather; book metadata; a jobs aggregator; trademark watch; recall search and recall join; company snapshot and company enrichment; CourtListener; EMA medicines; NADAC facts; cross-country statistics; carbon intensity and emission factors; EVM reads; prediction markets; Wikipedia answers and cited-answer search; CSE search; developer utilities; LLM inference; audio transcription; screenshot and render bundle services; Telegram provisioning; and Solana RPC via the RPC gateway. Added since the original submission is an agent-tooling and x402-verification suite: agent data core and data-core packs, discovery and matching, fee oracle, bridge and batch scoring, spend governance, transaction pre-sign checks, address verdicts, anomaly detection, toxicity scoring, source atlas, fact bank, ideas commons, power flags, publisher converter, resolution data, accessibility scans, A2A messaging, and Tron energy, plus the signed-verdict services for reputation, observability, prompt-injection screening, agent readiness, MCP manifest-claim audits, reference checks, tool-description ergonomics, API diffs, payload and token-efficiency checks, payability, settlement and receipt verification, x402 dialect conformance, quote-pair diffs, rail probes, ledger checks, challenge-lifecycle analytics, dispute-evidence packets, payment-chain reconciliation, failure translation, payload preflight, facilitator fit, true-cost and validity checks, rail routing, listing checks, execution receipts, quote registry, argument checks, stream divergence, subset-S reconciliation, artefact correspondence, trending digests, context audits, composite inventory and query index over cleared sources, demand verdicts, memory provenance and scope-hygiene audits, probe grading, recoverability probes, SDK version-safety, MCP auth-surface conformance, observatory aggregates, and wallet funding-and-gas readiness checks. The committed openapi.json is a snapshot of the live discovery document (https://exchangerebel.com/openapi.json); every paid operation carries x-payment-info pricing and a 402 response, and every service publishes a free catalog + /health so agents can plan spend before paying. Prices are USD per call as declared in each operation's x-payment-info, unless an operation's summary says otherwise (RPC is per 1,000-request block; Telegram provisioning is per bot-month; transcription is per minute of audio).
