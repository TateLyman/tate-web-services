## Manual Check Script

Before contacting, open the site on mobile width and write three facts:

1. What is hard to do on the page?
2. What could be fixed in under two hours?
3. What outcome does the owner care about: more calls, more quote requests, or less admin?

## First Offer

Lead with the $150 repair pass, not a full rebuild. It is easier to buy and easier to deliver. A good first reply can become a $350 rebuild or $250 automation after trust exists.
