# Lead Plan

## Best Targets

Prioritize businesses where a small web fix can visibly affect calls or bookings:

- Auto detailers and mobile car wash services
- Handymen, painters, landscapers, roofers, and small contractors
- Local wellness, fitness, tutoring, and coaching businesses
- Restaurants or food trucks with outdated menus or weak mobile pages
- Local service providers that rely on phone calls and quote forms

## Lead Criteria

Good fit:

- Website exists but looks outdated on mobile
- Contact button is hard to find
- Services and prices are unclear
- Google listing is active but website is weak
- Owner-operated or small team
- No obvious agency-built site

Bad fit:

- Franchise or national chain
- Already has a strong modern site
- No public email or contact form
- Medical/legal/financial claims that would require regulated copywriting
- Businesses that look inactive

## Daily Workflow

1. Find 10 local businesses in one category.
2. Open each website on mobile width.
3. Write 2-3 specific fix notes.
4. Send no more than 5 personalized messages.
5. Track replies in a spreadsheet with status: found, contacted, replied, quoted, won, lost.

## Pitch

The best first sale is the $150 repair pass. It is low commitment, easy to explain, and can turn into a $350 one-page rebuild after trust is established.

## Compliance

Keep cold messages truthful and one-to-one. Include your name and contact info. Do not send repeated follow-ups after someone says no.
